Getting here

By car, by plane or by bus

By car

You can drive up to the hotel entrance to unload your luggage. The old town has restricted vehicle access, so you'll need to move your car afterwards.

Parking

The hotel has two free parking spaces near the building, on a first-come basis and without the option to book. The rest of the old town is reserved for local residents, so it's best to leave your car in one of the free car parks nearby, five to ten minutes on foot.

From the airport

San Sebastián Airport is two kilometres away, within Hondarribia itself. It's a couple of minutes by car.

From San Sebastián and Irún

San Sebastián is 22 kilometres away and Irún six. Regular buses connect Hondarribia with both cities and with the French border.
