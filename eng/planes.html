Things to do

Things to do around Hondarribia

Sea, mountains, easy walks and a nearby towns worth the trip. Simple ideas to round off your stay, just steps from the hotel.

Sea

Hondarribia beach

The town's own beach, with a seaside promenade and views of Hendaye, across the bay. A short walk from the old town.

Cabo Higuer lighthouse

Where the Bidasoa river meets the Cantabrian Sea. Cliffs and wide views, at the northernmost point of the peninsula.

Mountain - Hiking

Sanctuary of Guadalupe

Starting point for the trails up Jaizkibel, with a viewpoint over Txingudi bay and Mount Larrun in the distance. A short, easy walk for anyone.

Jaizkibel coastal trail

The great natural lookout between Hondarribia and San Sebastián. Cliffside paths past the remains of old forts, always with the Cantabrian Sea in view.

Walks

The old town

Wander the cobbled streets, the old walls and Gipuzkoa square. The whole town can be covered in a short, unhurried walk.

Txingudi marshes

A flat walk along the estuary, between Hondarribia and Irún. A well-known area for its natural value and its birdlife.

Nearby towns

Hendaye (France)

Just across the border, with a long beach and a pleasant seafront walk. A few minutes away.

San Sebastián

Twenty-two kilometres from the hotel. For a full day out: La Concha beach, the old town and great food.

Biarritz (France)

Half an hour by car, already in France. Wide beaches and an elegant atmosphere, with the Grande Plage as its landmark.

Saint-Jean-de-Luz (France)

A charming French fishing town, a little closer than Biarritz. A good harbour and pleasant streets for a stroll.

Getaria

A fishing village known for its anchovies and txakoli wine, birthplace of the navigator Elcano. Close to Zarautz.

Motorcyclists and other plans

A good base for motorcyclists

The roads climbing Jaizkibel, winding along the cliffside, are well known among motorcyclists. And being right on the border, it's a great starting point for rides into France and the Pyrenees.

Birdwatching at Plaiaundi

A few minutes away, in Irún, the Plaiaundi Ecological Park is a protected bird sanctuary with several hides and free access. A great choice for anyone who enjoys nature at a slow pace.
