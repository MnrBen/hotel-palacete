The hotel

Inside the historic walls, in Plaza de Armas.

Hotel Palacete is located in the old town of Hondarribia. Step outside and you are already on the cobbled streets, minutes from the bars of La Marina and the harbour.

Hondarribia has one of the best preserved old towns on the Basque coast, with a seafaring atmosphere best enjoyed on an unhurried walk.
